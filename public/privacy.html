Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CloudSnap ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us, including:

  • OAuth and Account Credentials: Access tokens and account integration details for third-party platforms like LinkedIn to permit automated posting.
  • Uploaded Assets: Images and text posts that you upload to your S3 storage via our application interface.
  • Configuration Details: Custom API configurations, S3 keys, and other environment variables required to run the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Facilitate direct S3 image uploads.
  • Authorize and publish posts directly to your LinkedIn Personal Feed or Company Page.
  • Monitor application performance and troubleshoot errors.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your access tokens are stored securely in a local database and refreshed automatically. We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. However, please note that no system can be 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Integrations

CloudSnap integrates with LinkedIn APIs and AWS S3 services. Any data processed by these third parties is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@example.com.
